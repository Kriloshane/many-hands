We are not outside the landscape. We are part of it. We leave traces wherever we go, shaping the places we pass through. Nothing stays the same forever. Rain washes over stone, wind carries fallen leaves, and the ground slowly changes beneath our feet. Some traces disappear, while others remain long after their makers are gone. Nature does not separate the beautiful from the damaged, the living from the forgotten, or the permanent from the temporary. Everything exists within a landscape that is constantly changing. Our presence is only temporary, yet the traces we leave behind become part of the world around us. Not every mark needs to last, and not every change can be undone. The landscape is never finished. It continues to grow, shift and transform, carrying traces of everything that lives, passes through and leaves something behind.
