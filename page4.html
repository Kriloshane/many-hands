We forget that we started with art. Before paper and language, we spoke with earth and char. Through cave paintings we told stories, skills, dreams and shared what it meant to be alive. Society and capital deny us this now, this way of talking and understanding. It wants us to rely on machines rather than our eyes, hands, mouths and minds. If we accept; we sever ourselves from what it means to be human, beginning on those cave walls. We cannot let ourselves be isolated in the artificial. Art is communicating and understanding. And how we rebuild for all beings.
