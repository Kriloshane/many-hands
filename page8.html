***

I once photographed a scene in Richmond Park, London, capturing three deer beneath the sun. I later recreated this image on a stone in the park, allowing a moment that once existed in a photograph to return to the physical world in another form. Photography preserves a moment within an image, while painting allows it to leave the photograph and return to the natural world. The stone becomes a place to hold a memory. The deer and the sun are painted again on its surface, connecting a scene I once witnessed with the space as it exists today. We are always leaving traces in nature, while nature continues to change or erase them. For me, this work is an attempt to bring a memory back into the physical world. I want the image to become more than a photograph of the past. By becoming part of the natural landscape, it can exist here for a while before eventually giving the space back to the land.
